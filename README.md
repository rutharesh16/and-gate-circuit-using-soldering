**AND GATE CIRCUIT**

**1\. DESCRIPTION :**

**A simple AND gate circuit developed to understand basic digital logic and switching.**

**2\. PRODUCT USE:**

**AND gates are used in digital electronics, control systems, automation, and computer circuits where two input conditions must be satisfied.**

**3\. HOW WE MADE IT:**

**We used an AND gate, two push buttons, LED, resistor, battery and Dotboard. We arranged all the components and gave the required connections. The complete circuit was soldered and assembled by us to make it a working model.**

**4\. WORKING:**

**The two push buttons act as inputs.**

**Both inputs ON → LED ON**

**Any one input OFF → LED OFF**

**5\. CONCLUSION:**

**This project helped us understand the working of an AND gate, circuit connections, component assembly, and practical soldering.**
